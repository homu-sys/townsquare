About {{.SiteName}}

{{.SiteName}} is a small, self-hosted place to publish things: profiles, Markdown posts, threaded comments, emoji reactions, hashtags, and channels. It runs as one small Go service on your own hardware.

Avatars are resized and checked locally for adult or graphic content; nothing is sent to an external service. The NSFW channel stays hidden until you confirm the 18+ warning.

The public API starts at /api/v1/site. Writes use a personal token from settings. Version 1 hosts one town; Version 2 will connect towns with ActivityPub.
